DOT.

One look for the Brick paks Kino (video), Musicdisk (tracker music), Tuner (IPTV) and Speed. An evening ground in each app's own colour, dark glass cards, dot-matrix numbers, round keys. White shows where you are. One coloured dot shows what is happening. Every size on this page is in Brick pixels on the 1024×768 screen (about 400 ppi), and the C library uses the same numbers.

Principles

Six rules that decide most questions before they come up.

3.2 inches, 1024×768A palm-sized screen at about 400 ppi, at arm's length. Body text is 32 px (a 2 mm letter), nothing on screen is smaller than 24 px, and a screen holds one main thing.
White is where you areFocus and selection are always a paper fill with ink text. Nothing else on screen is paper-white.
Colour is what is happeningEach app has one accent. It appears only as a small dot for a state: playing, paused, resume, live, recording. The app's evening ground is mood, not meaning.
Dots for numbers and titlesDoto is for times, clocks, counts and screen titles. Names and sentences stay in Geist, labels in Geist Mono.
Every screen shows its buttonsThe dock along the bottom lists what the buttons do on this screen, at most four hints, and only ones that work.
Glass cards, not linesGroup things in dark glass cards on the evening ground. No borders, rules or boxes inside cards beyond spacing: the only edges are the glass's own light rim and the chips' outline.
2

Colour

Neutrals shared by every app; each app sets its own evening ground and accent (see Themes). The ground is a radial gradient from the light point, top-left by default, with a ±1.5 ordered dither against banding on the 6-bit panel.

3

Themes

Each app sets its look once with dot_set_theme(d, &DOT_THEME_*): an evening ground (three stops at the same lightness in every app) and an accent. The accents sit off the status red and green, so "playing" never reads as an error and "live" never as "connected".

Same glass and type, each app's ground and accent

Glass

Cards, list rows and the dock are charcoal glass over the ground: the app's colour comes through a little, so every screen feels like the same evening. It is "glass, but not glass": no blur, just a translucent body, a soft sheen and a light rim.

8

Layout

One grid for every screen: 40 px margin, a header band, a content area, a full-bleed dock. Four templates cover all three apps. Blue marks are measurements and never appear on the device.

Spacing steps: 8 · 16 · 24 · 32 · 40. Gap between cards 16, between rows 12. Overlay fades in 200 ms, hides after 3 s of play.

One screen per app

The same parts, three apps. Only the ground, the accent dot and the content change.

10

Motion

Things come and go the same way in every app: one ease-out curve, a few timings, nothing bounces. Motion says where something came from and where it went; it never makes you wait (a list settles in about 0.2 s, and buttons work while it moves). Screenshots are drawn still, where everything ends up.
