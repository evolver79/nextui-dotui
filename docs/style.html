One look for three Brick paks: Kino plays video, Musicdisk plays tracker music, Tuner plays IPTV. Grey ground, black rounded cards, dot-matrix numbers, round keys. White shows where you are. One coloured dot shows what is happening. Every size on this page is in Brick pixels on the 1024×768 screen, and the C library uses the same numbers.

Principles

Six rules that decide most questions before they come up.

3.2 inches, 1024×768A palm-sized screen at arm's length. Nothing on screen is smaller than 22 px, and a screen holds one main thing.
White is where you areFocus and selection are always a paper fill with ink text. Nothing else on screen is paper-white.
Colour is what is happeningEach app has one accent. It appears only as a small dot for a state: playing, paused, resume, live, recording.
Dots for numbers and titlesDoto is for times, clocks, counts and screen titles. Names and sentences stay in Geist, labels in Geist Mono.
Every screen shows its buttonsThe dock at the bottom lists what the buttons do on this screen, at most four hints, and only ones that work.
Cards, not linesGroup things in black cards on the grey ground. No borders, rules or boxes inside cards beyond spacing.

On the Brick the fonts ship as static files: Doto-Black.ttf (900), Geist-Regular / Medium / SemiBold.ttf, Geist Mono-Medium.ttf. All SIL OFL. Layout

One grid for every screen: 40 px margin, a header band, a content area, a dock. Four templates cover all three apps. Blue marks are measurements and never appear on the device.

Spacing steps: 8 · 16 · 24 · 32 · 40. Gap between cards 16. Overlay fades in 200 ms, hides after 3 s of play.
